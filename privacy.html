Privacy Policy

YouTube Autopilot (Daily Gaming News). Last updated: November 24, 2026.

Who this covers

YouTube Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Daily Gaming News channel to publish and measure that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool may access, for the owner's own YouTube channel only:

  • uploading videos and thumbnails, and changing the privacy, title or description of the channel's own videos;
  • reading the channel's own video list and public statistics (views, likes, comments);
  • reading the channel's own YouTube Analytics and Reporting data (for example watch time, retention, impressions and click-through rate).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's videos and to measure how they perform, so the channel can improve its future videos. It is stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not transferred anywhere except between the owner's computer and Google's YouTube APIs.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and revoking access

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for the channel and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers of the channel

The tool does not collect personal information about people who watch Daily Gaming News videos. YouTube's own analytics are aggregated and anonymous.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Daily Gaming News YouTube channel.
