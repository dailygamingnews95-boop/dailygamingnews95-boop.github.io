Privacy Policy

Daily Gaming News Uploader (TikTok). Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Who this covers

Daily Gaming News Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Daily Gaming News channel to send the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account as drafts. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, through TikTok Login Kit, the tool uses:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID and display name, to confirm which account is connected;
  • video.upload: permission to upload the channel's own videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

How it is used

Only to upload Daily Gaming News videos to the owner's TikTok inbox, where the owner reviews each draft and decides whether to post it.

Storage

TikTok's access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No other personal data is collected, and nothing is stored on any server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other people

The tool does not collect or process data about TikTok viewers or any other users.

Retention and removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services, and by deleting the stored token file. Tokens are deleted when the tool is no longer used.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Daily Gaming News YouTube channel.
